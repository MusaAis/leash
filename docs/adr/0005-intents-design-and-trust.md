# ADR 0005: Intents design and trust

**Status.** In progress. Built and unit-tested on branch `feat/p4b-contract`: routes, guardian, `approve_intent`, `spend_intent`, `unwrap`. Not run on any network. Nothing is deployed on mainnet.

**Context.** The vault holds native NEAR. NEAR Intents (1Click) swaps need a deposit of wNEAR to an address that 1Click returns per quote. The vault must not hand that choice to the agent.

**Decision.** The owner records routes (`dest_asset`, `recipient`, at most 10 per agent). A separate guardian account builds the 1Click request only from the vault's own data, checks the answer, and records a single-use approval (`approve_intent`) with the deposit account, the exact amount, an expiry of at most 30 minutes and a `quote_hash` (sha256 of the saved 1Click answer). The agent can only spend against a matching approval.

**Trust statement.** Routes are records, not enforcement: the vault cannot see a swap's destination. `quote_hash` is a guardian assertion for audit; the contract cannot check it.

**Consequences.**
- `spend` is enforced entirely on-chain; `spend_intent` is enforced on-chain for caps, pauses and approvals, but the link between a deposit address and the owner's route is checked by the guardian off-chain. The deposit address comes from 1Click. The guardian checks its shape (64 lowercase hex characters) and the contract refuses the vault, the owner, the guardian, any agent and the wNEAR account as a deposit account; nothing else ties the address to the route, and the quote signature is saved, not verified. A false answer can send the amount of one approval to another account without any key being stolen. The loss is bounded by the approval (at most the per-transaction cap, 5 open approvals per agent, 30 minutes) and by the daily cap. A compromised guardian key plus a compromised agent can do the same for any address inside the same bounds.
- Contract 0.2.0 changes the state layout. It is deployed fresh. There is no `migrate`.
- The storage deposit of `wrap.near` (0.00125 NEAR, minimum equals maximum) was read once on 2026-11-01 and is not re-checked by the contract.
- `spend_intent` checks run in this order: intents not configured, all paused, agent paused, zero amount, unknown route, over per-transaction cap, no matching approval (same route, deposit account and exact amount, unexpired, current epoch), over daily cap, insufficient funds. A caller without a policy is reverted before any check. The checks live in one function that takes all state as arguments.
- The daily cap counts the amount when the batch is scheduled and is never rolled back. The batch goes to wNEAR as one receipt (`storage_deposit` for the vault, `storage_deposit` for the deposit account, `near_deposit`, `ft_transfer`), so its actions succeed or fail together. If wNEAR changes its storage bounds the batch fails and the cap stays consumed. The two storage deposits are counted in the daily cap (see [ADR 0003](0003-spend-accounting.md)).
- One constant, `INTENT_OVERHEAD` (two storage deposits, 0.0025 NEAR), is used in the funds check (the available balance must cover the amount plus the overhead), the daily check and the daily counter. The same deposit is attached to both `storage_deposit` calls.
- `spend_intent` panics before counting anything when less than 120 TGas is attached; each batch action gets 10 TGas. These gas numbers are placeholders, to be measured in a rehearsal.
- `unwrap` (owner, 1 yoctoNEAR) calls `near_withdraw` on wNEAR with 1 yoctoNEAR and needs 30 TGas attached. It touches no cap and works while the vault or an agent is paused. The vault does not wait for the result: a failed `near_withdraw` leaves the `unwrapped` event on the chain.
- `wrap.near` source (the public NEAR core contracts, `near_deposit` and `near_withdraw`) was read once on 2026-11-02. Whether the deployed contract matches that source was not checked on the chain.
- The batch keeps the vault's own `storage_deposit` as its first action. `near_deposit` registers an unregistered caller itself but subtracts the storage minimum from the minted amount, so without that action the first swap would mint too little, the `ft_transfer` would fail, and the cap would stay consumed. The vault's own deposit is expected to be refunded when the vault is already registered (not yet verified on `wrap.near`; the rehearsal records the vault balance before and after). Until verified, the daily counter may over-count by 0.00125 NEAR per swap, which fails closed.
- `intent_sent` and `unwrapped` are written before the batch runs and there is no callback. They record the request, not the result. Proofs cite the wNEAR receipts and the vault balance.
